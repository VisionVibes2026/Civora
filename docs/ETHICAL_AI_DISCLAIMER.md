# Ethical AI, Legal Disclaimers & PII Protection Policy

## 1. Legal Advisory Boundary

**Civora is an Artificial Intelligence-powered Informational and Assistive Tool.**
- Civora is **NOT** a court of law, government registrar, or licensed legal practitioner.
- Answers, citations, and summaries generated by Civora are for **informational, educational, and representation-drafting purposes only**.
- While Civora applies statutory grounding and strict citation validation against cooperative acts and government notifications, official interpretations must be confirmed with the respective **Registrar of Cooperative Societies** or legal counsel.

---

## 2. No Automatic Government Filing
- Civora **does NOT submit, alter, or transmit legal grievances directly into official government portals or police records** on behalf of users without authorized e-sign authentication and explicit citizen consent.
- Grievance drafting generates a downloadable/printable representation draft that the citizen must review, verify for factual accuracy, sign, and submit through designated official channels (e.g. CPGRAMS, State Registrar grievance windows, or registered post).

---

## 3. PII Masking & Citizen Privacy
- Civora follows a **Privacy-First Architecture**.
- Identifiable citizen credentials (e.g. 12-digit Aadhaar numbers) are masked at the client and API gateway levels (`XXXX-XXXX-1234`).
- Document OCR processing operates strictly for metadata extraction and is subject to user data retention policies.
